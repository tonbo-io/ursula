Bootstrap:
  Each Raft group runs on three consecutive pods by node id (all pods when
  there are three or fewer), and the first of them initializes it once every
  voter reports it empty and no leader. A replica that ever held its group
  never initializes it again.

Static membership warning:
  server.replicaCount controls the initial voter set for a fresh cluster. Changing it
  on an initialized cluster is not safe Raft voter reconfiguration. Use the
  future Ursula operator for safe learner add, voter promotion, and voter removal.
